THE EL CIELO EXPERIENCE

A Hilltop Sanctuary
Above the Chaos of Town

Perched atop the ridge at Prabha Building in Dhanda/Totu, El Cielo Shimla (El Cielo Cumbre – "Heaven in the Hills") was born from a simple desire: to provide travelers a true mountain escape where silence replaces sirens and mist-clad valleys replace concrete corridors.

While central Shimla and the Mall Road bustle with vehicular jams and crowded sidewalks, Totu offers the pure, tranquil soul of Himachal Pradesh. Wake up to golden sunbeams pouring into your private balcony, sip warm freshly brewed ginger tea as clouds float beneath your feet, and unwind in an intimate, work-friendly environment where every guest is welcomed as family.

180° Panoramic Sunrise & Sunset Balcony Views

Unobstructed vantage points overlooking deep green pine valleys and snow-touched ridges. Watch dramatic evening color shows right from your room.

Authentic Home-Cooked North Indian Meals & Breakfast

Made fresh to order by caretaker Ujjwal. Piping hot aloo-pyaaz parathas with fresh butter, Pahadi masala chai, and hearty home-style thali dinners.

Work-Friendly & Peaceful Remote Sanctuary

High-speed stable Wi-Fi, comfortable desks, peaceful quietude, and power backup make El Cielo the preferred choice for mountain workations.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is safe parking available for cars & SUVs?

Yes! We provide complimentary on-site parking for our guests' private vehicles directly at the property.

How do we travel to Mall Road from Totu?

Mall Road is about 7-8 km away (~20-25 mins drive). Local taxis can be arranged by caretaker Ujjwal right to the Shimla Tourism Lift or Railway Station at standard fares.

Are room heaters provided in winter?

Yes, efficient electric room heaters are available to ensure complete warmth and comfort during chilly evenings.

What is the stair situation?

Rooms are located on upper floors accessible by stairs (no elevator), which grants unobstructed elevated panoramic views over the valley. Our team will gladly carry your bags!
